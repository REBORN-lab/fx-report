## Themes of the week

### Theme 1: the push from the dollar side reversed inside the covered span, and the four same-signed weakenings split into two groups (affects USD, EUR, PHP, THB, BRL)
**Macro backdrop**: in the first half of the span the dollar was reportedly approaching a two-week high with hike bets read as an endorsement, and all four local currencies weakened against the dollar with the same sign, each printing a range high; in the second half the dollar reportedly eased from that two-week high, bond yields softened and yen strength weighed on the dollar — the push reversed outright.
**Transmission**: softening short-end dollar yields → rate differential → the compensation for holding short-end dollar assets moves down and cross-border allocators shift positions back into local-currency assets → the exchange rate. The leg doing the work is the allocators' rebalancing, not the yields themselves.
**Target impact**: the euro's weekly change was -0.224%, the baht's -0.739% and the real's -1.168% — three moved net toward their own currencies across the span; the baht and the real printed the weekly range floors of 32.92 and 5.1114 respectively; the peso's weekly change was 0.405%, the only one net weaker against the dollar.
**Evidence strength**: it stands on four days — the hike-bet reports of 2026-09-01 and 2026-09-02, the yield attribution and the dollar's retreat on 2026-09-03, and the softening yields with yen strength on 2026-09-04. Contrary evidence is the peso: inside the same span it went the other way at 0.405% and never followed this layer on any day.
**Key assumption**: the US pair of readings is not replaced after the span — 3.365 (period 2026-07) and the 3.625 policy rate stay as they are through the CPI release of 2026-09-11; if that fails, the driver becomes a realised differential and this compensation-layer reading is void.
**Alternative reading**: the three currencies' recovery is spillover from money flowing back on the yen side, unrelated to the dollar's own compensation (its flip: at the next fixing the peso follows too and all four strengthen against the dollar with the same sign).
**Flip indicator (T+5)**: at the next fixing the euro breaks back above 0.86371 and all four resume weakening against the dollar with the same sign — once that appears, the dollar side's softening was only an episode at the end of the span.

### Theme 2: the peso is the only one that ran counter across the whole span; neither the hike nor the easing CPI turned it around (affects PHP)
**Macro backdrop**: inside the span July hot-money net inflows reportedly plunged 91% and the peso was called Asia's weakest link, after which it broke past 62 and printed successive record lows; on 2026-09-04 the policy rate was raised from 4.75 to 5.0 and CPI eased from 6.2 to 6.1, lifting the real rate to -1.1 — and that fixing still had the peso weaker against the dollar.
**Transmission**: the hike and the easing CPI together lift compensation to -1.1 → rate differential → compensation still sits on the negative side, so importers and local dollar debtors do not postpone their dollar buying for that notch → the exchange rate. The leg doing the work is that dollar buying, not the policy itself.
**Target impact**: the peso's weekly change was 0.405%, its weekly range 62.397–62.65, and the 62.65 at the end of the span is the weekly range high itself; it is also the only one of the four net weaker against the dollar.
**Evidence strength**: it stands on four days — the hot-money and "weakest link" reports of 2026-09-01, the record lows of 2026-09-02, the oil price and Middle East risk premium of 2026-09-03, and the hike with the easing CPI of 2026-09-04. Contrary evidence is the policy side on 2026-09-04 itself: the rate rise and the softer inflation should have delivered compensation, and the price did not absorb it.
**Key assumption**: the 5.0 policy rate (period 2026-08-28) and the 6.1 CPI (period 2026-08) are not replaced before the next release on 2026-10-07; if that fails, the point at issue reverts to the rate path itself and this theme lapses.
**Alternative reading**: the peso's counter-move is only a lagged transmission of the dollar side's softening and it will follow later (its flip: at the next fixing the peso moves with the other three toward stronger local currencies).
**Flip indicator (T+3)**: at the next fixing the peso falls back to 62.397 — once that appears, the negative-compensation layer has started to be absorbed and the compensation the hike delivered really did price in.

### Theme 3: the baht's adjustment share moved back from the exchange rate to the rate leg (affects THB)
**Macro backdrop**: in the first half of the span global bond rates were reportedly threatening the Thai market and the central bank warned that business suspensions endanger workers, with the baht printing the two range highs of 33.165 and 33.265; in the second half external rates softened, the baht turned, and on 2026-09-04 it moved -0.918% in one go.
**Transmission**: softening external rates → policy space → the local rate leg has stopped at 1.0 and given no more, so once the outside loosens, the adjustment share that had been carried entirely by the currency flows back to the rate leg → foreign differential positions cover the baht → the exchange rate. The leg doing the work is foreign differential positioning, not local policy.
**Target impact**: the baht's weekly change was -0.739%, its weekly range 32.92–33.265; the 32.92 at the end of the span is the weekly range floor itself, a full range away from the 33.265 high of the first half.
**Evidence strength**: it stands on four days — the two local reports of 2026-09-01 and 2026-09-02, the turn on 2026-09-03 and the -0.918% of 2026-09-04. Contrary evidence is on the local-indicator side: the 1.0 policy rate and the -0.947 real rate never moved across the span, so the covering did not come from local policy.
**Key assumption**: through the BOT MPC meeting of 2026-10-28, the 1.0 policy rate is not cut and -0.947 does not change again; if that fails, the policy-space layer has to be repriced entirely and this theme lapses.
**Alternative reading**: this notch of covering comes from a broad strengthening of regional currencies and has nothing to do with Thailand's own policy space (its flip: at the next fixing all four strengthen with the same sign toward their own currencies).
**Flip indicator (T+3)**: at the next fixing the baht breaks back above 33.265 — once that appears, the whole adjustment share has landed back on the currency leg.

### Theme 4: the euro area's hike narrative moved from "language" to "read as locked in", while the span's notch of recovery moved on the same days and in the same direction as the baht and the real — the narrative layer has not priced in on its own (affects EUR)
**Macro backdrop**: inside the span German inflation reportedly rose and euro-area inflation printed a three-year high of 3.3%, then came the ECB's own bank interest rate statistics, and by the end of the span a September hike was reportedly read as locked in with J.P. Morgan and BNP Paribas forecasting another in December; the contrary side is a reported unexpected drop in July retail sales.
**Transmission**: the hike path read as locked in → rate differential → expected short-end compensation in the euro area moves up, while the -0.693 negative real rate pushes the other way → both forces act on the holding cost of euro shorts at once → the exchange rate. The leg doing the work is that holding cost.
**Target impact**: the euro's weekly change was -0.224%, its weekly range 0.86044–0.86371; inside the span it first printed the high at 0.86371 and then retreated to 0.86044, and that retreat came on the same day and in the same direction as the baht and the real.
**Evidence strength**: it stands on four days — German inflation on 2026-09-01, the three-year high of 3.3% on 2026-09-02, the bank rate statistics on 2026-09-03, and the locked-in reading with the December forecast on 2026-09-05. Contrary evidence is the reported unexpected drop in July retail sales the same day, which points to the hike path being reopened.
**Key assumption**: the 2.25 policy rate and the 2.942874 CPI are not replaced before the ECB meeting of 2026-09-10; if that fails, the driver becomes the meeting itself and this theme lapses.
**Alternative reading**: the locked-in reading has been over-read — the reported unexpected drop in July retail sales points to the hike path being reopened, and the euro-area side will next deliver pressure in the other direction (its flip: at the next fixing the euro breaks back above 0.86371 and returns to the weekly range high).
**Flip indicator (T+3)**: at the next fixing the euro falls below 0.86044 while the baht and the real no longer strengthen alongside it — once that appears, the narrative layer has priced in on its own and that notch of recovery is no longer merely co-movement.

### Theme 5: the leg with the thickest carry cushion finally found a path of its own, and the new variable is tariffs (affects BRL)
**Macro backdrop**: in the first half of the span Brazil reportedly still topped the real-rate ranking after its cut and markets leaned dollar-strong ahead of COPOM, the Fed and the election, with the real printing up to 5.1989; in the second half the funding leg softened and the real strengthened at two consecutive fixings, and at the end of the span the strategic implications of US tariffs on Brazil were reportedly being assessed.
**Transmission**: softening yields on the dollar funding leg → rate differential → for carry positions funded in dollars, the lower funding cost lifts the realised value of the same 9.557 cushion → the entry side adds → the exchange rate. The leg doing the work is the entry side of those carry positions, and 9.557 is only its quote.
**Target impact**: the real's weekly change was -1.168%, the largest of the four; its weekly range is 5.1114–5.1989, and the 5.1114 at the end of the span is the weekly range floor itself.
**Evidence strength**: it stands on five days — the ranking report of 2026-09-01, the calendar report of 2026-09-02, the turn on 2026-09-03, the softening funding leg of 2026-09-04 and the tariff report of 2026-09-05. Contrary evidence is the tariff item itself: it lands on the terms of trade and delivers pressure opposite to the funding leg.
**Key assumption**: through the COPOM of 2026-09-16, the 14.0 policy rate is not cut another notch and 9.557 does not change; if that fails, the cushion thins and this theme's direction flips.
**Alternative reading**: the tariff layer will override the softening funding leg and turn the direction around (its flip: at the next fixing the real breaks above 5.1989 while the euro and the baht still strengthen toward their own currencies).
**Flip indicator (T+5)**: at the next fixing the real breaks back above 5.1989 — once that appears, the softening funding leg has been overridden by tariffs.
