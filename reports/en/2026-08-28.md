## Philippine peso (PHP)

At this fixing 61.902 made the 5-running-day range high itself, and the daily move of 0.391% is the largest of the four by magnitude.
**Transmission**: the hike lands while the currency still weakens → the improved compensation on the local short end has not bought any absorption → capital flows → the exchange rate; the bid comes from marginal buyers of local-currency assets, who did not come back because rates went up — the gap side overwhelmed the differential side.
**Already in the price?**: yes, and against the differential: the peso went to its range high on hike day, while the series still has the policy rate at 4.75 as of 2026-08-20 and the real rate of -1.45 has not moved — what is in the price is the "not enough" layer, not the improved-differential layer.
**Disagreement and judgement**: key assumption — the raised level does not move again before the next CPI print (2026-09-04), so -1.45, the deepest negative real rate of the five, remains this row's anchor (if not, the point at issue switches to the rate path itself). Alternative reading: the weakness comes from a unified push on the dollar side rather than a local gap (its flip indicator: all four move with the same sign and magnitude at the next fixing). Flip indicator: the peso falls back to 61.66 (T+3).
