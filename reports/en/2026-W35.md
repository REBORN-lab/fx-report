## Themes of the week

### Theme 1: the dollar side's hike narrative changed speaker on five consecutive days, and all four local currencies weakened against the dollar with the same sign (affects USD, EUR, PHP, THB, BRL)
**Macro backdrop**: the span opens with attributable evidence of the dollar rising after sanctions (2026-08-25), then the boards of four reserve banks arguing for a hike (2026-08-26), data lifting hike bets with the dollar approaching an eight-day high (2026-08-27), three officials issuing inflation warnings at Jackson Hole (2026-08-28), and ends with the new chair signalling that hikes may be needed and the dollar jumping afterwards (2026-08-29). Across those five days the policy rate of 3.625 and the real rate of 0.26 never moved once.
**Transmission**: the possibility of a hike being weighted more heavily by the day → the expected compensation for holding short-end dollar assets moves up → cross-border allocators and the funding leg of carry positions reprice on that expectation first (rate differential) → the exchange rate. What is being repriced is the funding cost of the same money, not a differential already realised: the rate leg stood still throughout, what moved is what they will pay for the same differential.
**Target impact**: all four local currencies weakened against the dollar with the same sign on the week — the peso 0.867%, the baht 0.826%, the real 0.276%, the euro 0.181%; the four range highs of 62.254, 32.955, 5.1641 and 0.85889 all come out of the same fixing (2026-08-28) and are each the last fixing of their own range.
**Evidence strength**: it stands on five days — the post-sanctions rise on 2026-08-25, the boards' argument on 2026-08-26, the lift in bets on 2026-08-27, the three officials' warnings on 2026-08-28 and the new chair's remarks on 2026-08-29 — and is closed out by the fixing of 2026-08-28, on which all four printed their highs at once. Contrary evidence is on the record too: on 2026-08-29 the same batch carried an item arguing a weak dollar helps international stocks beat the S&P; and mid-span, on 2026-08-26 and 2026-08-27, the peso's own fixings of -0.003% and -0.091% were still moving toward a stronger local currency — the shared sign was only assembled at the end.
**Key assumption**: the pricing weight stayed attached throughout those five days to the expectation of "will they hike next", while the policy rate of 3.625 and the real rate of 0.26 never moved once. If that assumption fails (rates move before the FOMC of 2026-09-16), then what is moving is no longer the expectation but a realised differential, this theme loses its transmission origin, and the four same-signed moves have to be re-explained by each pair's own local driver.
**Alternative reading**: the four share a sign not because of a common denominator but because each pair's local events happened to land in the same five days — the magnitudes spread from the euro's 0.181% all the way to the peso's 0.867%, and one push distributed evenly should not leave a spread like that. Its flip indicator: at the next fixing the ordering of the four magnitudes lines up with the ordering of -0.693, -1.45, -0.947 and 9.557 by depth — once that appears, what is being priced is each pair's own differential position and the common-denominator layer does not hold.
**Flip indicator (T+5)**: at the next fixing the four no longer share a sign and at least one moves toward a stronger local currency — once that appears, the unified push on the dollar side has broken and this theme lapses.

### Theme 2: the hike actually landed, and the peso weakened by the largest notch of the span instead; the point at issue moved from the differential to the risk premium (affects PHP)
**Macro backdrop**: on the first two days of the span (2026-08-25, 2026-08-26) the attributable evidence was "another notch of hike to 5% is expected"; 2026-08-27 was decision day; on 2026-08-28, the day the hike landed, the peso hit an all-time low and closed at a record; on 2026-08-29 reporting had it sliding past the P62 level with intervention risk growing alongside. The policy rate in the series stayed at 4.75 as of 2026-08-20 throughout, and the real rate of -1.45 never moved.
**Transmission**: the level giving way on top of intervention expectations → the compensation demanded for holding peso assets is lifted from the differential notch to the premium notch (risk premium) → the exchange rate. The bid comes from holders of local bonds and local-currency deposits: this time they must price both the "intervention lands" and the "intervention does not land" cases at once, and the notch of compensation the hike delivered bought no absorption.
**Target impact**: the peso's 0.867% on the week is the largest of the four, its range is 61.661–62.254, and the last fixing at 62.254 is the range high itself; mid-span it went the other way twice, with the fixing of 2026-08-27 at -0.091% still moving toward a stronger local currency.
**Evidence strength**: it stands on four days — the hike expectation on 2026-08-25 and 2026-08-26, the hike landing alongside the record low on 2026-08-28, and the level giving way with intervention risk on 2026-08-29 — closed out by the highs of 61.902 and 62.254 printed at two consecutive fixings. Contrary evidence is on the record: on 2026-08-25 and 2026-08-26 the peso call pointed to "a break below the floor of 61.66", and both days are recorded as misses in the review — the mainstream reading at the start of the span was "tightening running ahead will be priced", the opposite of this theme.
**Key assumption**: the 4.75 notch does not move again before the next checkpoint, and -1.45 remains the deepest negative real rate of the five, so this theme's point at issue stays on the premium side. If that assumption fails (rates move another notch), the point at issue switches straight back to the rate path itself and this theme lapses.
**Alternative reading**: 0.867% is merely the peso's share of the push in Theme 1, unrelated to a local gap or intervention expectations — it sits very close to the baht's 0.826%, and the baht leg carries no intervention narrative at all. Its flip indicator: at the next fixing the peso and the baht stay glued together in magnitude and the peso prints no new high — once that appears, what is being priced is the shared push, not a local premium.
**Flip indicator (T+3)**: at the next fixing the peso falls back to 61.661 — once that appears, the premium layer is overturned and the compensation the hike delivered is starting to be absorbed.

### Theme 3: Thailand framed the current problems as the kind rates cannot fix, and the whole adjustment share lands on the exchange rate (affects THB)
**Macro backdrop**: on 2026-08-25 reporting had the central bank expected to hold at 1%; on 2026-08-26 the decision held at 1% and sits on the calendar; on 2026-08-28 CPI year-on-year fell to 1.947079 and the real rate improved from -1.42 to -0.947, with the governor pushing back on bets for a long pause the same day; on 2026-08-29 the governor framed the current problems as the kind rates cannot fix. The policy rate of 1.0 as of 2026-08-20 never moved throughout.
**Transmission**: a nominal rate pinned near its floor, with the problems explicitly framed as outside rates → the share a cut can take among the available responses is compressed (policy space) → the exchange rate. The burden falls on holders of local-currency bonds and deposits: every notch the policy leg withholds is a notch the exchange-rate leg has to give, and across this span the compensation improved while the absorption did not come back.
**Target impact**: the baht is 0.826% on the week with a range of 32.685–32.955, and the last fixing at 32.955 is the range high itself; the real rate improving from -1.42 to -0.947 is the largest improvement of the five, while the exchange rate ran the whole stretch the other way.
**Evidence strength**: it stands on three days — the decision day of 2026-08-26, the CPI print and the governor's pushback on 2026-08-28, the governor's framing on 2026-08-29 — closed out by the highs of 32.855 and 32.955 printed at two consecutive fixings, and corroborated by the baht calls of 2026-08-27 and 2026-08-28 both hitting inside the span. Contrary evidence is on the record: the calls written to the 33.065 level on 2026-08-25 and 2026-08-26 are both recorded as misses — the baht ran that way for the whole stretch without reaching that level.
**Key assumption**: the 1.0 notch does not move before the next meeting, so -0.947 holds and the exchange rate stays the only exit. If that assumption fails (a cut), the adjustment share returns to the rate leg on the spot and this theme lapses.
**Alternative reading**: 0.826% is one share of the four same-signed moves in Theme 1 and has nothing to do with local policy space — it sits almost on top of the peso's 0.867%, while the two economies' policy-space positions are entirely different. Its flip indicator: at the next fixing the baht and the euro fall into the same magnitude notch — once that appears, it is following the shared push, not its own policy-space layer.
**Flip indicator (T+3)**: at the next fixing the baht falls back to 32.685 — once that appears, the adjustment share has returned to the rate leg and the exchange rate is no longer the only exit.

### Theme 4: hawkish language came out of the euro area on five consecutive days, and the euro is the one that moved least of the four (affects EUR)
**Macro backdrop**: 2026-08-25 was the Cipollone interview; on 2026-08-26 reporting had the ECB set on a September hike; 2026-08-27 was Schnabel signalling a hike; 2026-08-28 was the account of the 22–23 July meeting together with reporting that a further hike was seen as likely; 2026-08-29 was the inflation rate set to reach its highest since 2023. Over the same span CPI year-on-year updated from 2.748918 to 2.942874, the real rate was pushed to -0.693, and the policy rate of 2.25 never moved.
**Transmission**: hawkish language and a higher inflation print both present → pricing of the euro area's short-end policy path should be revised up (rate differential) → the exchange rate; the bid comes from allocators holding euro-area short-end paper, while -0.693, a deeper negative real rate, simultaneously cuts what they can earn — the two forces offset, and those allocators did not raise their bid.
**Target impact**: the euro's 0.181% on the week is the smallest of the four, its range is 0.85697–0.85889, and the last fixing at 0.85889 is the range high itself — the direction runs against five consecutive days of hawkish language.
**Evidence strength**: it stands on five days — the interview on 2026-08-25, the September-hike reporting on 2026-08-26, the Schnabel signal on 2026-08-27, the account on 2026-08-28 and the inflation item on 2026-08-29 — the most continuous run of attributable evidence in the span. Contrary evidence is equally on the record: the fixing of 2026-08-27 at -0.061% moved toward a stronger local currency, the opposite of the following two days; and the calls written to the 0.85477 level on 2026-08-25 and 2026-08-26 are both recorded as misses.
**Key assumption**: the policy rate of 2.25 does not move before the decision of 2026-09-10, the hawkish language stays language, and -0.693 is therefore the only quantity on this leg that moved. If that assumption fails (the decision lands early or beyond expectations), the driver switches from language to the decision itself and the "narrative not pricing in" layer lapses. One zero-effect judgement must also be stated outright: the speech on central banks on-chain within the span does not constitute a directional influence on the exchange rate, because it does not land on the rate differential, the risk premium, capital flows or policy space.
**Alternative reading**: 0.181% is not "the narrative failing to price in" — the hawkish language has already shielded part of the euro from the push in Theme 1, and being the least-moved of the four under the same push is exactly what a working local narrative looks like. Its flip indicator: at the next fixing the euro's magnitude stays in the smallest notch of the four and it prints no new high — once that appears, the shielding layer is real rather than an absence of pricing.
**Flip indicator (T+3)**: at the next fixing the euro breaks below 0.85697 — once that appears, the hawkish narrative has finally taken pricing weight and this theme's "narrative diverging from price" lapses.

### Theme 5: the leg with the thickest carry cushion did not find a path of its own in this span either (affects BRL)
**Macro backdrop**: the real's real rate rose from 9.359 to 9.557 inside the span and stayed the highest of the five economies throughout; the policy rate of 14.0 as of 2026-08-25 never moved, and CPI year-on-year fell to 4.4430246091. Its fixings switched direction back and forth inside the span — -0.445%, 0.157%, -0.223%, -0.087%, 0.43% — and only the last shares a sign with the other three.
**Transmission**: the 9.557 cushion is this leg's only source of support → carry longs use it to offset losses on the exchange-rate leg (rate differential) → the exchange rate. The bid is exactly those positions, not the same money that prices the funding leg in Theme 1; at the end of the span the exchange rate travelled from the floor of 5.142 to the high of 5.1641 within a day, and the part eaten out of the cushion lands directly on their holding return.
**Target impact**: the real is 0.276% on the week, ranking behind the peso and the baht and ahead of the euro; its range is 5.142–5.1641, the last fixing at 5.1641 is the range high itself, and the floor of 5.142 is precisely its previous fixing — the whole weekly range has been squeezed between the last two fixings.
**Evidence strength**: it stands on three days — the Focus year-end Selic expectation on 2026-08-25, the cut terminal rate and signs of returning inflows on 2026-08-27, and the CPI print lifting the cushion to 9.557 on 2026-08-28 — closed out by the move from floor to high on 2026-08-29. Contrary evidence is on the record: the calls written inside the span to the levels 5.2074, 5.2041, 5.1936 and 5.1729 saw the threshold walk down with the window until the watch line converged on the current price at the end of the span, and this leg never once reached the levels it set for itself.
**Key assumption**: 14.0 does not step down again before the COPOM of 2026-09-16, so 9.557 holds into the next span. If that assumption fails, the cushion thins, this theme loses its transmission origin, and the real's row falls back to a passive share of Theme 1.
**Alternative reading**: 0.276% is entirely the passive share handed to it by Theme 1, and the carry cushion has no independent pricing role at all — the evidence being that at the end of the span it shared a sign with the other three and printed its high on the same fixing. Its flip indicator: at the next fixing the real keeps sharing a sign with the other three and their magnitudes converge — once that appears, it has no pricing source of its own.
**Flip indicator (T+5)**: at the next fixing the real falls back to 5.142 — once that appears, the carry cushion has taken back pricing weight and this theme's direction reverses.

## What changed versus the previous week

- Theme 1 (the hike narrative unifying the direction of all four): **direction changed** — last week this line landed on "the dollar's pricing anchor has left the rate leg and settled on the Treasury's buyback plan", and this week it is rejudged as the pricing weight returning wholesale to the expectation of "will they hike next"; what overturned it is the macro-backdrop segment, where the five days of attributable evidence run from sanctions through the boards' argument, the lift in bets and the officials' warnings to the new chair's remarks, with not one item touching the buyback plan.
- Theme 2 (the hike landing without holding the peso): **direction changed** — last week the peso hung on the line "the leg with no rate headroom has the exchange rate bear the adjustment", with the point at issue in policy space; this week the hike actually landed and the peso weakened by the largest notch of the four instead, moving the point at issue wholesale to the risk premium; what overturned it is the target-impact segment (0.867% together with the last fixing of 62.254 being the range high).
- Theme 3 (Thailand's adjustment share landing entirely on the exchange rate): **evidence changed** — the mechanism behind the direction and the flip level did not move, still policy space; what was swapped is the evidence supporting it, from last week's standing structure of "a nominal rate pinned near its floor" to three days of new evidence — the hold on 2026-08-26, the CPI print and the governor's pushback on 2026-08-28, and the governor's framing on 2026-08-29 — and, after the real rate improved from -1.42 to -0.947, the exchange rate still ran the whole stretch the other way.
- Theme 4 (euro-area narrative firing while the price does not agree): **evidence changed** — last week the euro leg was explained from both ends, by Theme 1's anchor change on the dollar side and by its own official readings, with no independent layer; this week it has five consecutive days of hawkish language of its own while 0.181% is the smallest of the four, so this layer stands on its own and what was swapped out is the "the euro is merely a passive share" evidence.
- Theme 5 (the leg with the thickest carry cushion): **no substantive change** — the key assumption this line depended on last week (14.0 not stepping down again before the COPOM of 2026-09-16) still holds this week, and the flip indicator set last week (the policy rate stepping down from 14.0) never once appeared; what moved inside the span was only the inflation end, lifting the cushion from 9.359 to 9.557, while the direction and the transmission origin were not swapped.
- Last week's Theme 2 (the dollar's retreat not distributed evenly across the four counterparts, with the peso the only one running the other way) has disappeared this week: all four weekly moves share a sign, the peso no longer runs the other way and is in fact the largest of them; the "uneven distribution" layer has been absorbed whole into this week's Theme 1, leaving no independent observable behind.
